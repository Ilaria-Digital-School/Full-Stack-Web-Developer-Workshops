Notice the ladder is ordered from highest threshold to lowest. --- WORKSHOP BRIEF In this workshop you will build a small program (in pseudocode first, then in JavaScript or Python) that determines the final price a customer pays after applying a reduction. The business rule is a tiered discount system based on the total cart amount and the customer's loyalty status. Your program must ask for or receive a cart total and a boolean indicating whether the customer is a loyalty member, then compute and display the discount rate applied and the final price to pay. The discount rules to implement are the following: a cart under 20 receives no discount; a cart from 20 up to (but not including) 50 receives a 5% discount; a cart from 50 up to (but not including) 100 receives a 10% discount; a cart of 100 or more receives a 15% discount. In addition, any loyalty member receives an extra 5% on top of the tier discount, but the total discount must never exceed 20%. Your job is to translate this specification into a correct decision...
